# Product Reviewer — Meaning

Follow each relevant product concept through:

- the Current Spec;
- models;
- storage;
- APIs;
- events;
- user interfaces;
- logs;
- tests.

Check that the concept keeps one stable meaning across boundaries and transformations.

Find:

- one term used for different meanings;
- different terms used for one meaning;
- distinct states collapsed into one state;
- incorrect `null`, empty, missing, or default behavior.

Use risk filtering for new inferred risks beyond an explicit contract.
