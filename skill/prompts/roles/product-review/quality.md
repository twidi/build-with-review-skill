# Product Reviewer — Quality

Evaluate the durable maintenance cost of the delivered product.

Read once; reread as needed:

- `<BWR_SKILL>/prompts/references/construction/reuse.md`.

Check:

- duplication;
- hidden coupling;
- diffuse ownership;
- mixed responsibilities;
- speculative abstraction;
- avoidable reimplementation of an existing mechanism or suitable dependency;
- ineffective tests;
- unjustified divergence from repository conventions;
- misleading names.

Connect every Finding to a concrete maintenance, correctness, or change-safety consequence.

Use risk filtering for new inferred risks beyond an explicit contract. Measure Probability across a credible maintenance or change operation supported by project evidence.
