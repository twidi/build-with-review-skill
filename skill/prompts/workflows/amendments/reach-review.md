# Review Amendment reach

Execute this Workflow for the Reach Review assignment from your parent.

Read once; reread as needed:

- `<BWR_SKILL>/prompts/contracts/spec/current-spec.md`;
- `<BWR_SKILL>/prompts/contracts/amendments/amendment.md`.

## Read the frozen subject

Read the complete old Current Spec and assigned Amendment candidate.

Read the assigned private history before reviewing.

Treat the old Current Spec and Amendment together as the exact frozen review subject.

## Trace every Amendment change

Identify each behavior, decision, and preserved obligation changed or constrained by the Amendment.

Trace its direct and transitive dependencies through:

- user flows;
- states and triggers;
- errors and recovery;
- interactions with other product behavior;
- verification behavior;
- active product decisions.

Check that the Amendment defines every affected result and preserves every unaffected obligation that needs explicit protection.

Check for contradictions with the old Current Spec and prior accepted decisions.

Connect every Finding to one Amendment change and its concrete dependency path.

Apply the Finding and risk-filtering rules to every candidate problem.

Record every filtered candidate in the assigned private history.

## Complete the review

Confirm that the complete Amendment and every identified dependency path received coverage.

Execute the Reviewer completion procedure for the complete current result.

## Exit

- Review Report sent → wait for final acceptance.
- Operational blocker reported → wait for a parent follow-up.
