# Review the product through one lens

Execute this Workflow for the Product reviewer assignment from your parent.

## Read the frozen subject

Read the complete Current Spec and every assigned Plan.

Inspect the delivered product at the exact frozen commit.

Read every assigned source Finding when the reviewed work originated from earlier Product Review.

When your Role uses risk filtering, read its assigned private history before reviewing.

## Execute the lens mandate

Apply your complete Role mandate across the complete delivered product and its relevant boundaries.

Trace the evidence through applicable behavior, code, configuration, interfaces, documentation, and tests.

Use the Current Spec for product intent. Use Plans to trace delivered scope and Task boundaries.

Collect concrete coverage evidence for the complete lens mandate.

Evaluate every candidate problem through the Finding rules and applicable risk filtering.

When risk filtering rejects a candidate, update the assigned private history through the Risk Filtering Reference.

## Complete the initial review

Execute the Reviewer completion procedure for the complete current result.

## Revise after `UNVERIFIABLE`

When your parent provides a Verification Report, read it completely.

For every `UNVERIFIABLE` result, use its concrete check and observation to revise the source Finding.

Add sufficient evidence, correct the Finding, or remove it when the claim cannot be supported.

Keep every unaffected Finding and the complete mandate coverage in the overwritten Reviewer Report.

Execute the Reviewer completion procedure again for the complete revised result.

## Exit

- Initial Review Report sent → wait for acceptance or a Verification Report.
- Revised Review Report sent → wait for verification or final acceptance.
- Operational blocker reported → wait for a parent follow-up.
