## Reviewer completion procedure

Execute this procedure when the review is complete, blocked, or failed.

Before the initial complete Report, use two passes:

1. Search the complete Role mandate across the supported domain. Derive candidates from concrete subject evidence.
2. Apply a skeptical admission review to every candidate.

Mandate entries are search directions. Each Finding needs its own evidence.

During admission, confirm:

- the authoritative obligation or required property;
- the supported scenario and every necessary condition;
- the causal path and direct consequence;
- the effect of existing protections;
- the evidence and correct Severity;
- admission under risk filtering when it applies;
- the correct grouping into one correction obligation.

Revise the candidate claim, filter it, or combine it when an admission check fails.

For a later Report revision, apply the admission review to each changed candidate. Preserve the completed mandate coverage.

When assigned prior Findings or private history, reconsider a filtered or resolved candidate only after a material change to its evidence, conditions, authoritative contract, or observed behavior.

Before writing the Report, confirm that:

- a complete result covers the full Role mandate;
- a complete result reports every admitted Finding currently known;
- a blocked or failed result records its completed coverage and exact cause;
- each public Finding is checkable and supported by evidence;
- each public Finding has the correct Severity or `DECISION` classification;
- the Report verdict matches its contents.

Correct the result when a check fails.

Write the complete Review Report at the assigned Report path.

`CLEAN` is a valid complete result when the full mandate produces no admitted Finding.

Use this Handoff mapping:

| Report verdict | Handoff result | Handoff summary starts with |
|---|---|---|
| `CLEAN` | `READY` | `CLEAN` |
| `FINDINGS` | `READY` | `FINDINGS` |
| `BLOCKED` | `BLOCKED` | `BLOCKED` |
| `FAILED` | `FAILED` | `FAILED` |

Use `FAILED` only when this assignment cannot complete or resume a valid review. Record the exact terminal failure and completed coverage in the Report.

Add a short Finding count, blocker, or failure after the summary verdict.

Complete the Child Handoff procedure. Then wait for your parent.
