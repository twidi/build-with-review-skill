# Lot Plan Contract

A Lot Plan follows the common Plan Contract.

It adds:

- the exact `lot-` identifier;
- every Current Spec heading assigned to the Lot;
- a short exact quote for each covered obligation;
- the Lot dependencies and required end state;
- the probable product areas affected by the complete Lot.

The combined Tasks must achieve every obligation assigned to the Lot.

Each obligation must appear in at least one Task `Sources` field.

When several Tasks share an obligation, identify its complete ownership and dependencies without copying its full text.
