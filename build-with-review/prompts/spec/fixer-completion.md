# Completion block — fixer

**6 items.** Your report opens with this block, above the findings, and you paste it verbatim into your `parent` message. How to fill it: `completion-rules.md`.


    COMPLETION (6 items)
    - [ ] findings — N received, N applied, N declined
    - [ ] failure directions derived — N edits, N cases checked, N wrongly permissive, N wrongly restrictive
    - [ ] grep inventory — N identifiers grepped, N pre-edit hits, N edited hits; pre/post counts and reasons in the log
    - [ ] subdivision this round — what gained its own identity and how references were re-pointed, or n/a
    - [ ] self-detected items — N
    - [ ] blocked on a human decision — N

## For this mandate

- `blocked on a human decision` is load-bearing: a zero is a claim that you made no product choice on your own.
- `failure directions derived` reports both columns; a zero in either is a claim, not an omission.
- The per-identifier grep detail goes in the decisions log, where the next round's ripple checker reads it.
